Your world fell

Your ship is broken. The stars are still out there. Dig, build a home base, meet survivors and goblins, clear cursed keeps and swamp temples, and fly through an endless galaxy, all the way to strange alien worlds. Alone or with friends, in six languages.

How to install

Download the launcher for your computer and unzip it.
Start Orbit Horizon Launcher and press INSTALL. It downloads the game for you.
Press PLAY. From then on the launcher keeps the game up to date by itself.
Mac: the first time, right-click the launcher → Open → Open (it isn't signed by Apple yet).
Windows: if SmartScreen warns you, click "More info" → "Run anyway".
